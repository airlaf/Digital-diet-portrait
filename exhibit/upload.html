Connecting to the exhibition…

Get your Safari history
  1. On supported iPhones, open Settings → Apps → Safari → Export (below History and Website Data).
  2. Select only History and save the export to Files.
  3. In Files, tap the ZIP to extract it. Open the extracted folder and find History.json (it may include a profile name).
  4. Return here and choose that JSON file.

Menu names and availability vary by iOS version. If Export is unavailable, ask the host or try the sample portrait at the computer.

Apple’s export instructions
Maximum 10 MB. The first 10,000 titled entries are used.

Before you share

Common explicit words are automatically removed before sharing. This filter cannot catch every sensitive term; please review your preview. Your phone extracts page titles only. URLs and timestamps are not sent. Titles pass through the exhibition server’s memory and are cleared after the computer receives them, or within 15 minutes. The portrait clears after five minutes or when the host selects Next visitor.
